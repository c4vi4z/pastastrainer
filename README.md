# The Pasta Strainer Project  

Pasta Strainers: A Brief Summary

A pasta strainer, also known as a colander, is a kitchen tool designed to separate cooked pasta from boiling water. Its bowl-shaped structure is covered with small holes that allow liquid to drain away while keeping the pasta contained.

Most pasta strainers are made from stainless steel, plastic, or silicone. Stainless steel versions are typically more durable and heat-resistant, while plastic models are lightweight and affordable. Some designs include handles, feet, or extendable arms to improve stability and ease of use.

Although pasta is their namesake task, strainers are commonly used for washing fruits and vegetables, draining canned foods, rinsing grains such as rice, and preparing a variety of ingredients in everyday cooking.

In essence, the pasta strainer is a simple but highly effective tool whose entire purpose can be summarized in three words:

Keep the pasta.
 Lose the water.

A surprisingly elegant solution to a surprisingly recurring problem.
